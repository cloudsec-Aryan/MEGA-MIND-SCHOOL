Our story

Who we are

A sanctuary for intellectual curiosity

Mega Mind Sr. Sec. School, affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), is a co-educational day school situated near Goyal Petrol Pump on Bhiwani Road, Tosham Rural, Haryana.

Managed by Mahesh Mega Mind Shiksha Samiti, we opened our doors on 1 April 2005 and have grown into a senior secondary campus serving Nursery through Class XII — with over 800 learners and a dedicated faculty of 30+ teachers.

Our motto is simple: Where Learning Meets Possibilities. We believe every child can thrive when curiosity is met with care, structure, and opportunity.
