Curriculum

Learning that builds for life

We follow the Central Board of Secondary Education framework with strong foundations in languages, mathematics, sciences, and social studies — complemented by arts, sports, and value education.

Foundational & Primary

Nursery to Class V — play-based curiosity, literacy, numeracy, and socio-emotional growth in a safe kindergarten-friendly environment.

  • Phonics & Language
  • Number Sense
  • Art & Craft
  • Activity Periods

Middle School

Classes VI–VIII — conceptual depth, lab exposure, and project work that prepare learners for board-level study.

  • Science Labs
  • IT Skills
  • Library Hours
  • Clubs & Seminars

Secondary

Classes IX–X — focused CBSE board preparation with continuous assessment, remedial support, and exam readiness.

  • Board Mentoring
  • Subject Enrichment
  • Workshops
  • Career Awareness

Senior Secondary

Classes XI–XII — stream choices that open pathways to university and professional courses, guided by experienced PGTs.

  • Science Stream
  • Commerce Stream
  • Lab Practicals
  • Guidance Sessions
